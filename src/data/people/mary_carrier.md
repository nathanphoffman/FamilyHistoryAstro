## Biography

The pattern that runs down this side of the family — women raising children with
no father on the record — starts, as far back as anyone can see, with Mary
Carrier. Born in Tennessee somewhere between about 1814 and 1821, she had a
daughter, Jane, out of wedlock around 1840. Years later Mary married a man named
George Berry, and when Jane's own marriage fell apart and she was left with
nothing, it was Mary and George's household that took her in. The Carrier name
comes down this line through Mary alone; there is no man attached to it.

## Research Notes

Mary Carrier was born about 1814–21 and was the mother of Jane Carrier, who was
born illegitimate about 1840 in Tennessee. No father is recorded for Jane, so
the Carrier line runs through Mary alone.

She had married George Berry by 1870, and in that year her widowed daughter Jane
was living in their household.[^census_1870_carrier_berry]
