## Biography

Jacob Ipock (1693–1775) is where the family name changes shape. The tree records
him under both "Ipock" and its German original, "Eibach" — the sound of a
Palatine surname being flattened into something an English-speaking county clerk
could spell. Whether that anglicisation is a genuine piece of research or a guess
that hardened through repetition, nothing here can say. He is placed as the father
of Christian Micarroll Ipock, on the tree's word alone.

## Research Notes

Jacob Ipock is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the father of Christian
Micarroll Ipock, with the surname also recorded in its German form,
Eibach.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] The dates sit easily — about thirty-seven at his
son's birth — and rest on nothing but the tree.

Ipock as an anglicisation of Eibach is the sort of detail that is either a real
piece of research or a guess repeated until it looked like one. Nothing here
settles which.
