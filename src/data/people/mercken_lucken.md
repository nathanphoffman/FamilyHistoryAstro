## Biography

Mercken Maria Lucken (1652–1715) has come down as a wonderfully specific old
Dutch-German name and almost nothing else. The tree places her as the wife of
Wilhelm Streypers, in the Krefeld world that would soon plant Germantown,
Pennsylvania — but her dates aside, not a single fact of her life is recorded
here. The name outlasted the woman.

## Research Notes
