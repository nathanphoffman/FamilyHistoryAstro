## Biography

The name that runs three generations deep in this family begins with Uriah Stair
Wallick. Born in Pennsylvania in 1849 and living to eighty-six, he married Sarah
Ann Mundis and, with no way of knowing it, sent his first name down the line: his
grandson was christened Urie John, and his great-grandson Urie Jim after that. Of
the man himself the record has kept only the outline — the dates, the wife, and
the name he handed on.
