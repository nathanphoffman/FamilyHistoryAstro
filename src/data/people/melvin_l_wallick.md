## Biography

Melvin L. Wallick was the other half of the 1902 Wallick twins, born alongside his
sister Hanna in York County. He was among the first of John Ralph Wallick and
Myrtle May's five children, and roughly ten years older than his brother Urie
John. Like his twin, he survives in this history as little more than a name and a
year.
