## Biography

Justus Hubbell sits half in and half out of this family. Born, the tree says, at
Stamford, Connecticut, in 1732, he is kept on the chart as the father of Margaret
Hubbell purely because the dates leave room for it — while in the same breath his
wife is pushed out as Margaret's mother, because the dates for her don't work at
all. It is a strange spot for an ancestor to occupy: his fatherhood survives on a
technicality, and the profile behind it carries no source of any kind.

## Research Notes

Justus Hubbell is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the father of Margaret
Hubbell, born at Stamford, Connecticut, in 1732.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] The
Ancestry profile carries no source of any kind.

His marriage to Waitstill Bishop is dated 1766, nine years after the birth of the
daughter attributed to him here. His paternity is kept because the dates allow
it; her maternity is not, because they do not.
