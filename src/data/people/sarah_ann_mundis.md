## Biography

Sarah Ann Mundis (1853–1925) was a York County woman who married Uriah Stair
Wallick and, in 1878, had a son, John Ralph, at East Prospect. She and Mary
Elizabeth Stein — the other grandmother Urie John Wallick would descend from —
were both born in 1853, a full generation before their grandchildren John Ralph
and Myrtle married each other in 1901. Of her own seventy-two years, the record
has saved only the two dates.
