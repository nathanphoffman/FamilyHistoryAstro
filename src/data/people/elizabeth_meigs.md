## Biography

Elizabeth Meigs lived barely into her thirties — born around 1635, gone by 1664 —
and had her son Samuel at about twenty-two. On a stretch of the tree where most
couples' dates quietly contradict one another, hers are unremarkable in the best
way: they simply fit. That small internal consistency is very nearly all that can
be said for or against her.

## Research Notes

Elizabeth Meigs is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the wife of Richard
Hubbell Sr. and the mother of Samuel Hubbell.[^ancestry_tree_35916700]

She would have been about twenty-two at Samuel's birth in 1657 and died seven
years after it, which at least holds together — not something that can be said
of every couple on this branch.
