## Biography

John Ralph Wallick lived ninety-six years — from 1878, when York County still
moved by horse and rail, to 1975, the year a car was driven on the moon. He was
born at East Prospect, married Myrtle Edith May in the spring of 1901, and with
her raised five children: a set of twins in 1902, then Kenneth, then Urie John,
and last a son, Richard, in 1915.

He outlived a great deal. Richard died in 1937 at twenty-two, and Myrtle in 1966;
John kept going almost another decade past his wife, into his late nineties.

## Research Notes

John Ralph Wallick was born 14 February 1878 at East Prospect, York County,
Pennsylvania, a son of Uriah Stair Wallick and Sarah Ann Mundis. He married
Myrtle Edith May at York on 16 April 1901.[^wallick_may_marriage_1901]

Five children followed: the twins Hanna E. and Melvin L. in 1902, Kenneth May in
1903, Urie John in 1912, and Richard Ralph in 1915 — the last of whom died in
1937, in his father's lifetime.

He died 10 January 1975 at York, aged ninety-six, outliving his wife by nine
years.
