## Biography

Mary Magdalene Weddle — "Susan" in some tellings — is offered by the family tree
as a wife far up the Rouse line, born in Lower Saxony in 1738. The German origin
lends the branch some old-world colour, but the arithmetic is tight: she would
have been in her mid-forties when her son was born, which is possible and also the
kind of stretch that usually means a generation has gone missing from a
much-copied tree. Nothing documents her.
