## Biography

Mary E. Lester married John Quincy Alexander Stovall in Washington County,
Virginia, on an autumn day in 1863. Fifteen or so years later he left her, and she
raised the children on her own after that. She lived until 1906. Beyond the
marriage and those bare facts, the record has kept almost nothing of her — not
even a firm year of birth.

## Research Notes

Mary E. Lester (c.1841–1906) married John Quincy Alexander Stovall in Washington
County, Virginia, on 24 September 1863.[^wash_co_va_marriage_1863] The register
gives her age as 22 (consistent with an 1841 birth) and her birthplace as "Wilks"
— Wilkes Co., N.C., the same county as her husband's. It names her parents as
William C. Lester and Ann Lester, the only record found naming
them.[^wash_co_va_marriage_1863]
