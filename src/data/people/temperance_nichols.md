## Biography

For four years Temperance Nichols was known in the records simply as "Widow
Preston." Her first husband, Jehiel Preston, had died in 1684, and until she
married again she carried his name and his status. In April 1688 she wed Samuel
Hubbell, whose own wife had just died in childbirth, and the two half-households
became one. Born in 1662, she lived to about fifty-two. It is one of the more
plainly human stories on this otherwise thinly sourced branch.

Hubbell, and a daughter of Isaac Nichols and Margaret
Washburn.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] Her placement as David Hubbell's mother rests
