## Biography

Susannah Osborne is carried here as the wife of Isaac Jacob Lewis and the mother
of Philip Lewis, said to have died in Ashe County, North Carolina, in 1844, within
a year of her husband. Nothing has been found that actually ties her to Philip,
and the dates would have made her about forty-five when he was born — possible,
but only just. Like her husband, she is a name the tree supplies for a place the
records leave blank.
