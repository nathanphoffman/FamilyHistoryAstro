## Biography

Myrtle Edith May was born on Christmas Eve, 1878, in Lower Windsor Township, York
County, Pennsylvania. A few months short of her twenty-third birthday she married
John Ralph Wallick, in the spring of 1901, and over the next fourteen years she
had five children — twins first, then three more sons, the youngest in 1915. She
lived to eighty-seven, dying at York in 1966, nine years before her long-lived
husband.
