## Biography

This John Roe — born in Queen Anne's County, Maryland, in 1723 — has the bad luck
to share his exact name with his own son in the very next generation. Two John
Roes back to back is a standing trap: it is nearly impossible to be sure which man
a given record belongs to, and easy for a tree to fold the two into one. He is
given here as the father of the younger John Roe, with no source behind the claim
and that hazard hanging over it.

## Research Notes

John Roe, born in Queen Anne's County, Maryland, in 1723, is given by the family's
Ancestry tree as the father of the John Roe who married Nancy
Ipock.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] The Ancestry profile carries no source of any
kind.

Two men of the same name in consecutive generations is a standing invitation to
conflation, and this pair should be treated with that in mind.
