## Biography

Philip Isaac Rouse very nearly saw the whole of the nineteenth century. Born in
1808, reportedly in Lee County, Virginia, he lived until 1898 — from the age of
the flatboat and the flintlock to the age of the railroad and the telephone, some
ninety years. He was the father of Margaret Rouse, who married Philip Lewis in
1852.

Almost nothing else of that long life has survived. Everything the family tree
piles above him — a chain of Rouses and Surbers reaching back toward Switzerland —
rests on no record at all, which leaves Philip Isaac as the last reasonably solid
figure on this branch before it dissolves into guesswork.
