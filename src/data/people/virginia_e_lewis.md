## Biography

Virginia was Philip Lewis and Margaret Rouse's first child, born in the summer of
1859 in Washington County, Virginia, a year and a half before her brother George.
She appears twice more as a girl in her mother's household — a one-year-old in
1860, a ten-year-old in 1870, already fatherless — and then nowhere again. What
became of her as a grown woman has not been traced.

## Research Notes

Virginia E. Lewis was born 17 July 1859 in the Upper District of Washington
County, Virginia, daughter of Philip Lewis and Margaret
Lewis.[^va_births_1859_virginia_lewis] She is the "daughter Virginia, 1" of the
1860 census[^census_1860_washington_lewis] and "Virginia," age 10, in her widowed
mother's household in the 1870 census.[^census_1870_washington_lewis]

She had previously appeared in this project's notes only as a name on the
family's Ancestry tree, attached to no record.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] The birth
register and both censuses now put her on firmer footing than that. Nothing is
recorded of her life past 1870.
