## Biography

Katharine Streypers is a dateless name carrying a famous one. She appears only as
the mother of Barbara Page, with no birth or death recorded, but her surname ties
her to the real Streypers brothers of early Germantown. As elsewhere on this
branch, the name being genuine does nothing to make the descent genuine.

## Research Notes

Katharine Streypers is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the mother of
Barbara Page.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] No dates are recorded for her.

Streypers, like Kunders, is a genuine early Germantown name — the Streypers
brothers were among the Krefeld purchasers of 1683. The same caution applies: the
name being real does not make this descent real.
