## Biography

Kenneth May Wallick, born in York County in 1903, carried his mother's family name
in the middle of his own — a common Pennsylvania way of keeping the maternal line
in view, here the Mays of Lower Windsor Township. He was the third of John Ralph
and Myrtle Wallick's children and an older brother of Urie John. Nothing further
about him has been set down here.
