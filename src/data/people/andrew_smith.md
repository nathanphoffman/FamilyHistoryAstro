## Biography

Andrew Smith surfaces once, in the 1870 census of the Glade Spring District — a
Black man with a wife, Sallie, and a houseful of children, in a southwest Virginia
county only five years out of slavery. After that the record loses him completely.
He is in this history only as the father of William Smith, one of the men weighed
as a possible father for Alice Stovall.

## Research Notes

Andrew Smith appears in the 1870 census of the Glade Spring District with his
wife Sallie and their children, his race recorded as Black and hers as
Mulatto.[^census_1870_smith] Nothing further about him has been established. He
enters this research only as the father of William "Will" Smith.
