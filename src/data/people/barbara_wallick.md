## Biography

Sometime in the last couple of decades, Barbara Wallick — Barbara Galka by
marriage, the second daughter of Urie John Wallick and Lorene Lewis, born in the
late 1930s — mailed off a saliva sample to one of the consumer DNA companies. The
result that came back, as the family understood it, showed more than a trace of
Native American ancestry, which she took to be Cherokee.

That small, modern errand is what reopened a question more than a century old. The
report itself has never been passed around or examined — no company, no date, no
percentage is written down anywhere — but it was enough to send the whole family
back into the records, looking for where such a thing could have entered the line.
