## Biography

Linjntjen "Ellen" Teissen Doors owns one of the most vivid names anywhere in this
family — and one of its emptiest entries. Given as the wife of the Germantown
founder Thones Kunders and the mother of Henry Cunreds, she carries a birth year
of 1650 and a death in 1729 and, beyond that, nothing. As with her husband, the
Germantown history behind the name is genuine while the line down to this family
is not shown.
