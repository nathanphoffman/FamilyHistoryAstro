## Biography

Margaret Wexdale is a name almost too rare to chase. Born, the tree says, in 1708,
with no year of death recorded, she appears only as the wife of Jacob Ipock and
the mother of Christian — about twenty-two when that son was born. "Wexdale" shows
up so seldom in the records that it is hard to tell whether it is a real surname
badly spelled or a spelling error that turned into a surname.
