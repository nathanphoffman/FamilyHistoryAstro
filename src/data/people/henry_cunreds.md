## Biography

Henry Cunreds (1688–1758) hangs on his own misspelled surname. "Cunreds" is
almost certainly a worn-down form of Kunders or Conrads — a real and recurring
name in early Germantown — and it is that echo, more than any record, that lets
the family tree slot him in as the son of the Germantown founder Thones Kunders
and the father of Barbara Page. A surname that merely sounds like a famous one is
a thin thread to hang a branch from, and here it is the only thread there is.

## Research Notes

Henry Cunreds is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the father of Barbara
Page of Germantown.[^ancestry_tree_35916700]

The name is very likely a garbling of Conrads or Kunders. Thones Kunders was one
of the thirteen Krefeld families who founded Germantown in 1683, and the name
recurs there for generations. That makes the claim plausible in shape and no
better evidenced for it — a real Germantown surname is exactly what an
unsourced tree would reach for.
