## Biography

William Marshall Roark married Maggie Stovall in the summer of 1894. His whole
significance to this family is a question of timing: the wedding came eight years
after Maggie's daughter Alice was born, which is the plainest proof that whoever
Alice's father was, it was not him. He arrived in Maggie's life too late to be the
answer everyone keeps looking for.

## Research Notes

William Marshall Roark married Maggie Stovall on 16 August
1894.[^wash_co_va_marriage_1894] The register gives his age as 36, widowed,
born Ashe Co., N.C., parents Wm. & Patsy Roark; the ceremony was performed by
E. F. Kahle. He appears in this tree as Maggie's husband only — the marriage
postdates Alice's 1886 birth by eight years, so he is not a direct ancestor of
Lorene Lewis.
