## Biography

Missouri was the youngest of Philip Lewis and Margaret Rouse's four documented
children, born in Washington County, Virginia around 1867 — likely close to her
father's death, since her mother was already a widow heading the household by
the time Missouri turns up in the 1870 census as a three-year-old. Like her
sisters Virginia and America, she carries a map-drawn name and then disappears
from the record.

## Research Notes

Missouri Lewis appears as "Missouri," age 3, in her widowed mother Margaret's
household in the 1870 census of Washington County, Virginia, alongside her
siblings Virginia, George and America.[^census_1870_washington_lewis] No birth
record has been found for her; her birth year is estimated from that census age,
and agrees with the family's Ancestry tree's estimate of
1867.[^ancestry_tree_35916700]

She had previously appeared in this project's notes only as a name on that tree,
which attaches no record.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] The 1870 census now confirms
her as a real member of the household. Nothing is recorded of her life past
1870.
