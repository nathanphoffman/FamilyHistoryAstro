## Biography

Noah Kidd married into this family in 1871, taking as his wife the recently
widowed Margaret Rouse and, along with her, a household of Lewis children — among
them young George, the boy this line runs through. He is not a blood ancestor; his
part in the story is as the stepfather who stepped into a hard gap, at a time when
a mountain widow with children could not manage long on her own.

## Research Notes

Noah Kidd married Margaret Rouse in 1871, within a few years of the death of her
first husband, Philip Lewis.[^marriage_1871_kidd_rouse] He appears in this tree
as Margaret's second husband only; George W. Lewis and his siblings were
children of the first marriage.
