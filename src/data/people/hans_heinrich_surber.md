## Biography

Hans Heinrich Surber is where the family tree turns exotic: a man said to have
been born in Zürich in 1719, the lone Swiss note in the whole ancestry. It is a
lovely detail and a suspicious one — a foreign birthplace is exactly the sort of
thing that gets copied faithfully from tree to tree without anyone ever pulling
the emigration record that would prove it. Here, no such record exists. The Zürich
birth is a claim, and so far only a claim. The tree gives him a wife, Barbara
Page of Germantown, and a son, Adam Newell Surber.
