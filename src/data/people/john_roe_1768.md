## Biography

John Roe was born in the Carolina mountains in 1768, while the colonies were still
British, and lived until 1860, on the very eve of the Civil War — ninety-two
years, if the tree has it right, spanning nearly the whole life of the young
republic. He married Nancy Ipock in 1794 and is placed as the father of Margaret
"Peggy" Rowe — "Rowe" and "Roe" being one name in two spellings, which is exactly
the seam where this branch was stitched onto the rest of the family.

## Research Notes

John Roe is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the father of Margaret "Peggy"
Rowe, marrying Nancy Ipock on 27 February 1794.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] The
Ancestry profile carries no source of any kind.

Rowe and Roe are the same name differently spelled, which is ordinary for the
period and is how the two halves of this branch were joined in the tree.
