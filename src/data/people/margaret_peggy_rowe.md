## Biography

Margaret "Peggy" Rowe exists in this history mostly to fill a blank. The mother of
Margaret Rouse was never set down in any record, and Peggy is the name the family
tree offers for that empty space — born, it says, in 1811, died in 1871, married to
Philip Isaac Rouse. The lone date attached to that marriage is impossible on its
face, which is not encouraging. She may well be the right woman; there is simply
nothing yet to show it.

## Research Notes

Margaret "Peggy" Rowe is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the wife of
Philip Isaac Rouse and the mother of Margaret Rouse.[^ancestry_tree_35916700]
Her daughter's mother has never been identified from any record, so this fills a
slot that the documented tree leaves empty.

The tree dates the marriage to 1801, which cannot be right: her husband was born
in 1808 and she in 1811. The marriage is carried here without a date rather than
with an impossible one.
