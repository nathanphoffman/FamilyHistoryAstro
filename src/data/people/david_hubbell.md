## Biography

David Hubbell (1698–1753) comes with a title the family tree awards him and no one
has ever earned on his behalf: "Lt. Col. Commanding." Colonial Connecticut kept
careful records of its militia commissions, so the rank would be easy enough to
confirm or puncture — but nobody has looked, and until someone does, the colonel
is decoration rather than biography. He is carried here as the father of Justus
Hubbell, on the tree's word alone.

## Research Notes
