## Biography

Alonzo Peter Ipock (1750–1800) has one thing going for him that much of this
branch does not: his dates actually fit. He would have been about thirty-one when
his daughter Nancy was born, a perfectly ordinary age, with no arithmetic
straining anywhere. That quiet plausibility is also the whole case for him —
beyond it, he is a name the tree supplies and no record confirms.
