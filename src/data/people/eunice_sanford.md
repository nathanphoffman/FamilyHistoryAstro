## Biography

Eunice Sanford has no dates at all — no birth, no death — and yet a rather grand
pedigree is left dangling behind her: the family tree trails a line of Sanfords
and Bulkeleys back to a well-known clerical family of Concord, Massachusetts. The
catch is that the tree never actually says those people are her parents; the
connection is read off a shared surname and a spot on the page. She is carried
here as the wife of David Hubbell and the mother of Justus, with the impressive
ancestry marked as a proposal rather than a fact.

## Research Notes

Eunice Sanford is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the wife of David Hubbell
and the mother of Justus Hubbell.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] No dates are recorded
for her.

A Thomas Sanford and a Hannah Stevens sit one generation above her in that tree,
and behind them Ezekiel Sanford, Rebecca Bulkeley of the Concord clerical
family, James Stevens and Mary Wakeley. The tree never states that Thomas is
Eunice's father. Reading it off the shared surname and the position would be a
guess, and a guess drawn as a line is indistinguishable afterwards from a fact,
so they are recorded here as proposals instead.
