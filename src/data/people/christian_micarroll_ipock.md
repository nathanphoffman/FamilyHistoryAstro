## Biography

Christian Micarroll Ipock (1730–1782) carries a middle name odd enough to be a
gift to anyone who wanted to trace him — "Micarroll" is not the sort of thing that
gets duplicated by accident, and it ought to be findable in the old Craven County,
North Carolina, records. So far no one has gone looking. He sits in the tree as
the father of Alonzo Peter Ipock, a placement that fits the dates and rests on
nothing else.
