## Biography

Richard Hubbell Sr. is the ceiling. Born in 1626, dead in 1699 at Bridgeport,
Connecticut, he is the oldest name this family's tree carries on the Hubbell side
— the point where the branches simply stop and there is nothing above him at all.
The dates are at least consistent with his standing as Samuel Hubbell's father,
and that consistency is the only thing holding him in place.

## Research Notes

Richard Hubbell Sr. is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the father of Samuel
Hubbell, dying at Bridgeport, Connecticut, in 1699.[^ancestry_tree_35916700]

He stands at the top of the Hubbell line as this chart carries it. The dates fit
— he would have been about thirty-one at Samuel's birth — and nothing else
supports them.
