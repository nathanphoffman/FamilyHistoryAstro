## Biography

Obediah Sams can be followed through the record for half a century — not by
letters or diaries, but by land. Born in Tennessee around 1787, he turns up in
Sullivan County deeds from 1807 all the way to 1856, buying and selling ground,
his name entered in the county's books again and again. For its time and place, it
is a solid, visible life.

The one thing the deeds cannot prove is the connection that puts him in this
family at all: he is the likely father of Hiram Sams, but "likely" is as far as it
reaches.

## Research Notes

Obediah Sams was born about 1787 in Tennessee and is the probable father of
Hiram Sams. He himself is solidly documented — he appears in the 1850 census of
Sullivan County,[^census_1850_sullivan_tn] and his name runs through local deeds
from 1807 to 1856, half a century of recorded land
dealing.[^sams_deeds_1807_1856]

What is not documented is the link to Hiram. It should be treated as a working
hypothesis rather than an established connection.
