## Biography

Mary Elizabeth Stein (1853–1933) married Henry Aloysius May and had a daughter,
Myrtle, in Lower Windsor Township in 1878. She outlived Henry by fourteen years
and reached eighty herself. Past the marriage, the daughter, and those dates, the
record has kept nothing of her.
