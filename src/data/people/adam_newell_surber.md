## Biography

Adam Newell Surber is the grandest figure the family tree lays claim to — and one
of the least proven. He is written down as a physician, the only professional man
anywhere in these generations, born at Germantown, Pennsylvania, in 1751 and
dying eighty-two years later, far away, in Pulaski County, Kentucky. In the shape
of the tree he is the great connector: the join between the Swiss Surbers on one
side and the colonial Connecticut Hubbells on the other.

Not one document here backs any of it. The doctor, the dates, the Germantown
cradle and the Kentucky grave all come from copied online trees and nothing else.
He may have been exactly the man described; for now he is a very impressive
sentence with no record beneath it.
