## Biography

Urie Jim Wallick was the baby of the house — the third child of Urie John and
Lorene, born around 1948, and the third generation to carry some form of the name
that began with his great-grandfather Uriah Stair Wallick. When the census taker
came through York in the spring of 1950, he was two years old and written down
simply as "James," listed beneath his sisters Colleen and Barbara in the family's
rented home. That line is, for now, the only dated trace of him in this history.

## Research Notes

Urie Jim Wallick was a son of Urie John Wallick and Lorene E. Lewis, and the
third generation to carry a form of the name that came down from Uriah Stair
Wallick.

The 1950 census gives him a date for the first time. In the Wallick household at
house 603 in York, below Colleen and Barbara, is a son recorded simply as
**James**, white, male, aged 2, never married, born in
Pennsylvania.[^census_1950_york] An age of 2 in April 1950 puts his birth in
about 1948.

The identification rests on the household rather than on the name. This chart
already held a son of Urie and Lorene called Urie Jim, and the census shows
exactly one son, of the right family, in the right place. Jim is the ordinary
short form of James, and a boy named after his father would very plausibly be
entered by the name he was called. It is not proof: a fourth child named James,
with Urie Jim born later or dead before 1950, would fit the same page. But one
son in the household and one son in the tree is the reading taken here.

A FamilySearch index of this page assigns him the surname Stevens. The image
shows him under the ditto marks of the Wallick household, so the index is wrong.
