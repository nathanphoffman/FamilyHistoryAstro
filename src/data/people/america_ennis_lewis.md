## Biography

America was the third of Philip Lewis and Margaret Rouse's children, born in
Washington County, Virginia in the early 1860s and carrying — like her sisters
Virginia and Missouri — one of the map-drawn names the family favored that
decade. She was about six years old, fatherless, in her mother's household at
the 1870 census. Nothing further about her life has been traced.

## Research Notes

America Lewis appears as "America," age 6, in her widowed mother Margaret's
household in the 1870 census of Washington County, Virginia, alongside her
siblings Virginia, George and Missouri.[^census_1870_washington_lewis] No birth
record has been found for her; her birth year is estimated from that census age.

She had previously appeared in this project's notes only as a name on the
family's Ancestry tree, which gives her birth year as 1862 and calls her
"America Ennis Lewis," and attaches no record.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] The 1870
census now confirms her as a real member of the household, though a year or two
later than the tree's estimate. Nothing is recorded of her life past 1870.
