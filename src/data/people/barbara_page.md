## Biography

Barbara Page may not really belong to this family at all. She appears far back on
the Surber side as a wife said to have come from Germantown, Pennsylvania — but
her surname matches neither of the parents assigned to her, and a name that fits
nothing around it is exactly what a mistaken connection tends to look like. Maybe
she was married once before; maybe the tree has simply stitched two unrelated
people together. Until a record surfaces, she is a name holding a place that may
or may not be hers.

## Research Notes

Barbara Page of Germantown is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the wife of
Hans Heinrich Surber and the mother of Adam Newell
Surber.[^ancestry_tree_35916700]

Her parents are given as Henry Cunreds and Katharine Streypers, which raises an
obvious problem: her surname matches neither. That is not impossible — a first
marriage would explain it — but a surname that fits nothing around it is also
what a bad graft looks like.
