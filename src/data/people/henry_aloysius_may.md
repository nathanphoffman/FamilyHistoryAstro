## Biography

Henry Aloysius May stands on the threshold where this branch of the family becomes
simply American. Born in Pennsylvania in 1854, he married Mary Elizabeth Stein and
had a daughter, Myrtle, who married into the Wallick family in 1901. His own days
were ordinary enough that they left almost no mark.

What makes his page worth a pause is one line in the 1900 census: his father had
been born in Germany. That is the only foreign birth anywhere in the Wallick and
May lines — meaning the immigrant who crossed the ocean and began the American
chapter is Henry's own father, standing just out of frame and still without a
name.
