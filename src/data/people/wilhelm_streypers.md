## Biography

Through Wilhelm Streypers, the family tree reaches for a second of Germantown's
founding families in a single stroke. The Streypers brothers, like the Kunders,
were among the thirteen Krefeld households that founded Germantown, Pennsylvania,
in 1683 — and the tree has this line descending from both, through one husband and
one wife. In a settlement that small it is not impossible; it is also exactly what
a growing tree does when the biggest names in the neighbourhood are within reach.
His daughter Katharine has no recorded dates at all, so there is not even an
internal check to run.
