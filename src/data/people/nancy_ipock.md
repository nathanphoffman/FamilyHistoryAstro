## Biography

The dates around Nancy Ipock refuse to cooperate. Born in Craven County, North
Carolina, around 1781, she is said to have married John Roe in early 1794 — which
would make her thirteen — and the same tree then dates her own parents' wedding to
1786, five years after she was born. Girls did marry very young, and children were
born before weddings, so neither is outright impossible; but two contradictions
stacked on one shaky date are usually the date's fault. If this branch is ever
worked seriously, Nancy is where to begin.
