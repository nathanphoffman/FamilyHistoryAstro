## Biography

Isaac Nichols — "Issac," as the tree misspells him — is one of the oldest names
the family carries, born in 1617 and living to 1695. He appears only as the father
of Temperance Nichols, to whom he would have been about forty-five when she was
born: on the older side for the time, but nothing unusual. Beyond that placement,
he is a name and two dates.

## Research Notes

Isaac Nichols is given by the family's Ancestry tree as the father of Temperance
Nichols, and spelled "Issac" there.[^ancestry_tree_35916700] He would have been
about forty-five at her birth in 1662 — on the high side, and well within
possibility.
